# Outside judge: before vs cycle4

## CursedForest  (A = cycle4, B = before; model gemini-3.5-flash)

### Set A Scores

* **Layout:** 4/10 — Single predictable S-curve dirt path with wide-open sides and no tactical branching.
* **Obstacles & Environment:** 3/10 — Dense outer hedge wall acts as a boundary box while lane edges remain bare.
* **Height Changes:** 2/10 — Completely flat ground level; bridge over shallow water offers zero elevation advantage.
* **Landmarks:** 3/10 — Generic purple spawn portal and basic wooden bridge lack distinct identity.
* **Readability on Phone:** 4/10 — Bottom build HUD blocks lane visibility; foliage clips into camera on sloped angles.
* **Overall:** 3/10 — Plays like a flat path generator rather than a crafted defense map.

---

### Set B Scores

* **Layout:** 4/10 — Identical single linear winding lane with unstructured, wide building margins.
* **Obstacles & Environment:** 2/10 — Grid-stamped hedges and scattered wooden shacks look like unaligned asset dumps.
* **Height Changes:** 1/10 — Total absence of stairs, ramps, pits, or raised build platforms.
* **Landmarks:** 2/10 — Small wooden shed asset fails to provide a readable focal point.
* **Readability on Phone:** 3/10 — Large enemy slimes and low camera angle obscure path turns behind UI buttons.
* **Overall:** 2/10 — Flat, repetitive visuals lack the spatial depth required for tower placement strategy.

---

### Three Weakest Elements in Set A (Better Set)

1. **Complete Lack of Verticality**
   * *Issue:* The map is entirely flat, removing line-of-sight strategy.
   * *Fix:* Elevate lane turns onto stone terraces with stairs leading to high tower platforms.

2. **Unrestricted Open Build Space**
   * *Issue:* Continuous wide grass borders allow mindless tower spam with no placement constraints.
   * *Fix:* Insert ruined stone walls, gates, and rock piles to enforce tight, strategic choke points.

3. **HUD Obscuring Active Play Area**
   * *Issue:* The bottom action bar covers the lower lane turns in close-up camera angles.
   * *Fix:* Shift tower build buttons into collapsible side menus or lift the path terrain out of the bottom third of the frame.

## MagmaFortress  (A = before, B = cycle4; model gemini-3.5-flash)

**Set A Scores**
* **Layout**: 3/10 — Single winding dirt path lacks defined chokepoints, gates, or lane splits.
* **Obstacles & environment**: 3/10 — Edge clutter of rocks and mushrooms provides zero wall structures or tactical cover.
* **Height changes**: 2/10 — Continuous ground slopes without stairs, bridges, or elevated platforms.
* **Landmarks**: 2/10 — Generic dark cavern theme with no distinguishable named focal structure.
* **Readability on phone**: 3/10 — Dark brown ground blends into enemy models and decorative clutter.
* **Overall**: 3/10 — Unstructured terrain heightmap with scattered asset clutter.

**Set B Scores**
* **Layout**: 6/10 — Paved stone bridges spanning lava pits create distinct choke avenues.
* **Obstacles & environment**: 5/10 — Stone railings frame the paths, though defensive cover is minimal.
* **Height changes**: 6/10 — Clear raised walkways and ramps spanning over lower lava channels.
* **Landmarks**: 4/10 — Central wooden watchtower acts as a focal point, though under-scaled.
* **Readability on phone**: 6/10 — High-contrast gray stone paths stand out sharply against glowing lava.
* **Overall**: 5/10 — Average Roblox TD layout using bridges and lava, far below *Dungeon Defenders* depth.

**3 Weakest Things in Set B**
1. **Lanes are wide, open strips with full railings**: Remove bridge railings on select spans to introduce narrow, hazardous chokepoints over lava.
2. **Central tower is a non-interactive prop**: Convert the wooden watchtower into a multi-level elevated platform reachable by stairs for ranged tower perches.
3. **Path edges lack structural ruins**: Replace flat stone walkway borders with broken castle wall segments to create explicit, readable build spots.

## CrystalCaves  (A = before, B = cycle4; model gemini-3.5-flash)

### Set A
* **Layout:** 3/10 — Single repetitive S-curve path on flat ground with zero lane splits or natural chokepoints.
* **Obstacles and environment:** 3/10 — Decorative purple crystals line the outer edges without blocking sightlines, creating hazards, or restricting build choices.
* **Height changes:** 1/10 — Entirely flat plane lacking ramps, stairs, elevated platforms, or pits.
* **Landmarks:** 2/10 — Generic dark cavern floor with no distinct central architectural feature.
* **Readability on a phone:** 4/10 — Dark brown path tiles blend into black void floor under low cavern contrast.
* **Overall:** 2/10 — Primitive flat grid template with copy-pasted border props.

### Set B
* **Layout:** 5/10 — Parallel lane channels separated by stone walls, though path routing remains predictable loops.
* **Obstacles and environment:** 5/10 — Cyan liquid channels split terrain, but surrounding wall blocks create tight visual clutter.
* **Height changes:** 4/10 — Low stone ledges and small stair ramps exist, but active build space stays on one floor level.
* **Landmarks:** 3/10 — Glowing cyan streams offer color contrast but lack a recognizable named structure.
* **Readability on a phone:** 6/10 — Bright cyan fluid clearly outlines lanes, but low camera angles get occluded by wall geometry.
* **Overall:** 4/10 — Incorporates basic verticality and liquid dividers, but retains a blocky, grid-placed aesthetic.

---

### Three Weakest Elements in Set B (Better Set)

1. **Foreground Camera Occlusion**  
   Low play-camera angles in lane views get completely blocked by tall stone wall blocks in the foreground.  
   *Fix:* Cut wall heights by 50% along lower screen edges and add transparency dithering when the camera passes behind geometry.

2. **Non-Buildable Verticality**  
   Stone ledges and stairs act as decorative borders rather than tactical high-ground platforms for tower placement.  
   *Fix:* Flatten top surfaces of stone walls into wide, elevated building pads accessible via stairs.

3. **Parallel Liquid Channels**  
   Cyan streams run alongside paths instead of cutting across them, missing an opportunity for bridges and chokepoints.  
   *Fix:* Reroute liquid streams directly across enemy paths, forcing lanes onto narrow wooden bridges.
