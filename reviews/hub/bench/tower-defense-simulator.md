### Part C: Ratings (1–10)

* **Hub (6/10):** Looks pleasant on top, but incomplete map geometry leaves gaps where players can stare directly into the void [02:31].
* **Towers (7/10):** Well-proportioned character models that sit cleanly on map surfaces [08:31].
* **Monsters (6/10):** Functional path-following humanoids, though lacking visual flair in short clips [08:31].
