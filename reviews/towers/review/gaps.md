# Four towers: the three biggest gaps per tower (review 2026-11-24, no repainting)

Scores: scores.md (scripts/match_score.py from the painting's angle). Pictures per level: <kind>_l<n>_sheet.png
(painting | model), _turntable16.png, _in_game_phone.png.

## Flame Cannon (IoU 90.3 / 87.5 / 84.7 %, colour dE 14.7 / 15.6 / 14.9)
1. Level 3 has one barrel; the painting has two barrels side by side on a tall cone-shaped boiler with a chimney
   and glowing arched windows. The model's base is a low drum, so level 3 hardly reads as an upgrade.
2. The barrels read as woven brick or wicker (striped paint); the painting's barrels are smooth bronze-brown with
   a few broad gold bands. Level 1 is grey iron wrapped in thick gold rope on a thin A-frame on a plank.
3. Fire: the painting's bores hold flames; level 1's bore is a flat orange disc, the coal tray on level 2 reads as
   blue-grey balls, and level 3's smoke is lilac instead of grey-white.

## Frost Crystal (IoU 89.8 / 87.6 / 85.0 %, colour dE 12.5 / 11.8 / 11.9)
1. Level 3's crown is much smaller and lower: the painting has a tall silver arch with big crystals rising high
   round a floating, glowing orb; the model has a low ring and a flat blue disc for the orb.
2. The body is a straight cylinder with brick-like wooden strips; the painting's is a tapered stone drum with
   dark planks and gold corner brackets. Level 3's stone turns pink.
3. Snow and ice: the painting's snow caps and icicles are heavier; level 1's banner shows a small figure where
   the painting has the snowflake.

## Storm Obelisk (IoU 86.4 / 82.1 / 77.1 %, colour dE 15.7 / 17.3 / 19.8)
1. Level 3's orb is a flat purple disc with no glow or lightning arcs; in the painting it is the brightest thing,
   a crackling sphere held in two horns.
2. Shape: the painting's obelisk tapers to the top and stands on a gold-trimmed stone skirt with arched doors; the
   model's is a straight block on a ring of wooden planks, with a wide flat orange cap.
3. Level 1's standing stone is an egg with a faint small rune; the painting's is a chunky rock with a big glowing
   bolt between two wooden posts with gold bands.

## Ballista (IoU 75.4 / 68.3 / 85.1 %, colour dE 18.1 / 22.5 / 13.2)
1. The bow is missing on every level: no bow arms and no string, only the stock, so it reads as a log on a stand.
2. Level 3 has one small ballista on top; the painting has two big ones on a wide, squat round tower. The
   model's tower is narrower and taller.
3. Level 2's winch wheel, iron feet and gold caps are missing or small; the stock is painted pale and striped
   where the painting has dark wood with a red-feathered bolt.
