### Part C: 7 Art Direction Rules for New Pet Games

1. **Maintain Chibi Ratios:** Keep head-to-body proportions between 1:1 and 1:1.5 across all base models.
2. **Cap Shape Counts:** Build base pet rigs using no more than 5–10 simple geometric primitives.
3. **Restrict Palette Complexity:** Limit standard pet models to 2–4 flat colors to prevent visual clutter.
4. **Scale Eyes for Readability:** Map facial eye textures so they occupy 15–20% of the total head face width.
5. **Standardize UI Framing:** Display inventory previews from a uniform 3/4 isometric angle on neutral cards.
6. **Tier Higher Rarities with Shaders:** Reserve emissive glow effects and animated color cycles exclusively for top-tier pets.
7. **Keep Hatching Snappy:** Limit unboxing reveal animations to under 3 seconds using simple particle bursts.
