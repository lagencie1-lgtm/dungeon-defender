# Goblin King (boss): in a real run, round 2 (2026-11-26)

Filmed at phone size and alone on the path. Shots: walk from the side and 3/4, bash, the boss tells, hit and knock out.
Before the hits it gets enough health to survive them, so the fall has its own shot. `overview.jpg` holds the first row of each sheet; `numbers.txt` holds the measurements.

- **Grades (1-10):** walk 7 / slam 7 / charge 7 / hit 6 / knock out 7
- **Measured:** Slam: top -21%. Charge: rears up, then crouches, top -26%. Bash slide 0.075 per contact (round 1: 0.27-0.80), target under 0.05 not quite met. Turning at a corner still slides a foot 0.5-0.65 studs on some contacts.
