### Part A: Lobby / Hub Evaluation

1. **Layout and size:** A compact, open circular night-resort hub bounded by futuristic neon arches and city skylines [00:17, 09:43]. Spanning roughly 100–150 studs across, walk times between stations are short.
2. **First 5 seconds:** Upon loading, players face the main arena portals [00:17, 09:43], though immediate vision is blocked by intrusive event/shop popups [00:00].
3. **The Landmark:** The massive, glowing neon "SUMMON" stage framed by vibrant tropical archways [00:50, 09:43].
4. **Match start:** Players enter large glowing teleporter portals labeled "PLAY" [09:43] or use party matchmaking panels [00:56, 02:40]. UI screens display map names like "Planet Greenie", wave counts, and party multipliers [00:52, 02:31].

6. **Lighting and colour:** Saturated night palette dominated by deep blues, purples, and high-contrast cyan/pink neon highlights [00:17, 09:43].
7. **Life:** Populated with player avatars displaying glowing cosmetic aura effects [03:07], pulsing light displays, and particle trails [03:22, 09:43].

- **Towers:** Standard anime character models placed on streets, lawns, or rooftops [00:44, 01:34, 03:02]. Red/green grid boxes clearly define valid placement zones [02:56].
- **Monsters:** Mobs follow ground street paths [00:44, 06:09]. Readability degrades significantly as waves progress due to extreme particle spam [01:04, 01:53].

### Part D: 5 Hub Features Small TD Games Should Copy

1. **Massive Overhead 3D Signage [09:43]:** Place 20+ stud wide glowing neon text above major portals so players instantly find game modes without opening menus.
2. **Compact Radius Layout [00:17, 09:43]:** Keep all main features within a 150-stud circle around spawn to eliminate dead walk time.
3. **Screen-Edge HUD Anchoring [00:17]:** Lock inventory and shop hotkeys to screen margins, leaving the central view unobstructed for navigation.
4. **Dedicated Centerpiece Summon Stage [00:50, 09:43]:** Turn the gacha mechanic into a major architectural landmark to create a visual centerpiece for the hub.
5. **Direct NPC Interaction Prompts [04:43]:** Use clearly marked NPC interact prompts at crafting/reroll stations instead of burying functions in generic screen menus.
