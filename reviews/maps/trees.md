| Score out of 10 | A | B |
|---|---:|---:|

B still has three major flaws:

1. **The foliage is too noisy.** Bright yellow-green flecks compete across every crown, making the forest look crunchy. **Fix:** Reduce the small bright marks and paint broader, quieter patches of colour.

2. **The lighting lacks direction.** Highlights appear scattered rather than consistently facing a light source. **Fix:** Establish one light direction; warm the upper-facing masses and deepen the undersides with cooler greens.

3. **The crowns crowd together.** In the overview, foreground trees merge into a dense green wall, making individual silhouettes hard to read. **Fix:** Vary crown heights and widths, and leave deliberate gaps between groups so trunks and branches remain visible.

## Judge (blind: A = NEW, B = OLD; codex gpt-6.1-terra)

**A is better overall.** Its crowns have more varied, angular leaf masses and read more like stylised foliage. B is fuller, but its repeated round shapes and bright highlights make the forest look like a pile of shiny green balls.

| Criterion | A | B |
|---|---:|---:|
| Painted look | 6/10 | 3/10 |
| Shape | 6/10 | 4/10 |
| Light | 5/10 | 4/10 |
| Fit | 7/10 | 4/10 |

A’s three biggest remaining flaws:

1. **The crowns repeat too much.** Similar clustered shapes make neighboring trees feel copied. Create several distinct crown profiles: tall and narrow, broad and flat, and irregular or broken.
2. **The lighting is patchy rather than directional.** Bright yellow-green spots appear across the crowns without a consistent shadow side. Establish one light direction and group darker, cooler foliage underneath and on the opposite side.
3. **Some crowns sit like caps on trunks.** Branches are often hidden or end abruptly at the foliage. Add a few visible, tapered limbs that fork into the major crown masses, especially on foreground trees.
