# Pets Go: pets

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JRClaaYZXJc (Gemini model gemini-3.5-flash)

### Part A: Pet Construction & Design

1. **Body Recipe:** Built from simple cubic primitives [00:11]. Most pets feature a single blocky body/head unit (1:1 ratio) with either no legs or minimal stubby feet [00:28].
2. **Face:** Eyes are circular or rounded squares, occupying ~20% of the head width [00:11, 00:19]. Faces are painted directly onto the front texture map with simple mouths, noses, and cheeks [01:22].
3. **Surface:** Flat vibrant colors with occasional gradient highlights on rare tiers [02:39]. Standard pets use 2–4 colors with smooth, unlit shading and soft outer glows [01:02].
4. **Rarity Differentiation:** Higher-tier pets feature larger scales, attached accessories like hats/wings [01:02, 02:44], animated surface textures, surrounding particle auras, and custom hatch cutscenes [08:58, 22:37].
5. **Motion:** Pets bounce and hop rhythmically during idle and movement states [00:05]. Hatch reveals feature an egg shaking [05:44], a bright flash at 05:48, and a burst explosion displaying the pet at 05:52 (~8-second sequence).
6. **Menu Display:** Rendered from a straight or slightly angled front perspective on clean white or gradient tiles with clear text overlays showing odds and names [00:14, 15:10].

### Part B: Harsh Ratings (1–10 Scale)

*Scores are compressed toward the center per evaluation guidelines.*

* **Silhouette: 5/10** — Basic cubic foundations make base silhouettes identical without distinct accessories.
* **Face Appeal: 6/10** — High-contrast, friendly expressions effectively target a young audience.
* **Form Quality: 6/10** — Extremely clean primitive geometry with no visible mesh errors or clipping.
* **Surface: 5/10** — Bright, readable colors, though basic pets rely heavily on simple flat textures.
* **Rarity Read: 6/10** — Top-tier pets stand out clearly through scale, particle effects, and extra geometry.
* **Set Cohesion: 7/10** — Uniform blocky geometry creates a very consistent family style.
* **Motion and Life: 6/10** — Energetic hatch sequences and snappy hopping animations add good polish.

### Part C: 7 Copyable Design Rules

1. **Standardize Base Mesh:** Construct every base pet using a single 1x1x1 cube block to guarantee style consistency.
2. **Key Eye Proportions:** Draw eye features at exactly 18–22% of total head width for optimal cute appeal.
3. **Strict Color Palette:** Limit standard pets to 3 base colors; reserve gradients exclusively for rare tiers.
4. **Scale High-Tier Variants:** Increase "Huge" rarity models to 300–400% the size of standard pets.
5. **Time the Reveal:** Keep hatch reveal animations strictly between 6 and 8 seconds to maintain excitement without wasting time.
6. **Cap Accessory Count:** Add no more than 2 distinct geometric add-ons (e.g., horns, wings) per rare pet to prevent clutter.
7. **Uniform Inventory Framing:** Render all UI preview thumbnails at a fixed 15-degree isometric angle.
