# Outside judge: before vs cycle3

## CursedForest  (A = before, B = cycle3; model gemini-3.5-flash)

**Set A**
* **Layout**: 3/10 - Flat, single-track S-curve with zero natural chokepoints.
* **Obstacles and environment**: 2/10 - Copy-pasted border wall of identical green sphere trees reeking of lazy procedural placement.
* **Height changes**: 1/10 - Completely flat ground plane with zero stairs, pits, or raised platforms.
* **Landmarks**: 2/10 - Generic purple end portal and basic wooden hut give zero distinct identity.
* **Readability on a phone**: 6/10 - High contrast between brown dirt and green grass keeps pathing clear, despite visual monotony.
* **Overall**: 3/10 - Flat field with a path, lacking any professional layout design.

**Set B**
* **Layout**: 5/10 - Water bodies and stone bridges force actual chokepoints along the lane.
* **Obstacles and environment**: 5/10 - Stone walls, arches, and water channels introduce real physical barriers.
* **Height changes**: 4/10 - Minor stone steps and slightly raised bridge decks provide minimal elevation.
* **Landmarks**: 4/10 - Central stone arch bridge over water acts as the only recognizable anchor.
* **Readability on a phone**: 5/10 - Extra stone, wood, and water textures introduce clutter on a small screen.
* **Overall**: 5/10 - Average Roblox map that barely touches basic Dungeon Defenders mechanics.

**Three weakest things in Set B**
1. **Repetitive outer border**: the dense wall of identical green tree spheres round the outside looks like unedited
   auto-generated foliage. Fix: sculpted stone cliffs and ruined castle ramparts instead.
2. **Shallow verticality**: bridges and stone steps barely sit above ground level. Fix: raise platforms several
   metres with steep stairways and elevated perches.
3. **Ambiguous build zones**: unclear where towers can be placed on narrow walkways and uneven banks. Fix: flat
   stone pedestals that mark valid tower spots.
