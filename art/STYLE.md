# Art style: one set for the whole game

Every painted image in the game (icons, portraits, item art, key art) follows this sheet, so the set reads as
one game. The prompt prefix below is used for every image in `art/make_icons.py`.

**Look:** painted fantasy, warm and friendly (for 9-13 year olds), not dark or gory. Soft painterly shading,
bold readable silhouette, light from the top-left, a gentle rim light.

**Palette:** dark wood `#46301E`, brass `#D6AA50`, cream `#F3E6C8`. Each item adds one accent colour:
gems cyan `#5ADCFF`, mana violet `#AA96FF`, gold yellow `#FFD246`, fire orange `#FF7A2A`, frost blue `#8CD2FF`,
storm purple `#9678FF`, nature green `#5FB45A`.

**Icons:** one object, centered, 3/4 front view, fills about 80% of the square, transparent background,
no text, no frame, no drop shadow box. Must read at 48 px on a phone.

**Never:** emoji look, photo-real, glossy 3D-render plastic, thin lines that vanish at small size, words or
numbers in the image, real brands or characters from other games.
