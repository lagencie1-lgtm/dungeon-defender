### Top 3 Flaws of Set B & Fixes

1. **Repetitive Silhouettes**
   - *Flaw:* Canopies use the exact same stacked shape across the board.
   - *Fix:* Randomly scale, rotate, and break up foliage sub-meshes to vary tree outlines.

2. **Harsh Ground Transitions**
   - *Flaw:* Trunks plunge straight into the ground without natural blending.
   - *Fix:* Add flared root geometry and moss or grass tuft assets around trunk bases.

3. **Uniform Color Palette**
   - *Flaw:* Foliage greens are monotone across shadows and highlights.
   - *Fix:* Tint top highlights warmer (yellow-green) and push inner shadows cooler (blue-green).
